About the Programme

What Sets Us Apart?

The Curriculum Design followed in our B.Sc. (Hons) Economics program equips the students with the right skill set for core research and analytical work profiles in Economics, Finance and Management. It is a perfect balance of theory and application where the students are trained in wide variety of subject basket, including the core economic subjects, management, finance, and quantitative subjects. The design of curricula is to develop analytical and quantitative abilities, problem-solving aptitude and cognitive thinking. The academics here are research oriented and research projects and case studies constitute an important element of pedagogy. Regular interactions with eminent experts from Industry, academia and policy circles gives the students the edge over their peers in analysing the economy problems and find adequate solutions.

The Exposure for students as part of the programme is designed to provide meaningful engagement with both the corporate and public sectors through regular interactions with industry professionals, public policy experts, and practitioners from diverse fields. Industrial visits offer students firsthand insights into organisational processes, business operations, and workplace practices, while NGO visits and community engagement drives enable them to understand social and developmental issues through direct field experience. The programme also includes internships that provide students with valuable professional experience and help them apply classroom learning in real-world settings. Guest lectures, workshops, and expert interactions cover a wide range of contemporary themes including public policy, climate economics, entrepreneurship, sustainable development, labour markets, financial markets, and economic development, enabling students to engage with diverse perspectives and practical challenges beyond the classroom.

The Assessments methodology here is broadly divided into Internal evaluation components and external evaluation components with equal weightage. Internal assessment includes case studies, worksheets, research papers, and Internal Semester Assessments (ISAs) based on multiple-choice questions, while the external semester assessment is a written examination designed to evaluate students through application-oriented questions across subjects. Case studies enable students to connect theoretical concepts with real-world economic situations and develop practical problem-solving skills. Research papers introduce students to the research process, strengthening their ability to identify problems, analyse data, interpret evidence, and communicate findings—skills that are valuable for careers in areas such as market research and equity research. Mini Projects form an integral part of the programme, providing students with opportunities to undertake guided research and, where appropriate, develop their work for publication in collaboration with faculty mentors. These projects strengthen students’ writing, research, analytical, and data interpretation skills while enabling them to build a strong academic and professional portfolio.

How We Teach

Pedagogy

01

Case-Based Learning

  • Each semester includes a substantial number of real- world cases focusing on various economic, business, and policy issues. This case-based approach allows students to engage in critical thinking, apply economic theories to practice, and develop a strong understanding of complex market dynamics, pricing strategies, risk analysis, business expansion, and government policies.

Worksheets and Assignments

  • Worksheets are regularly assigned to reinforce concepts taught in class. These are designed to enhance students’ grasp of quantitative and qualitative economic methods, helping them practice problem-solving techniques and apply their learning to data analysis and economic modelling.

Research Papers

  • The program emphasizes academic research, with students required to produce research papers each semester. These papers focus on contemporary issues in economics, encouraging students to explore topics like policy analysis, macroeconomic trends, and the impact of globalization. Research papers help students hone their writing, analytical, and research skills, preparing them for potential roles in academia or research institutions.
02

Guest Lectures

  • The program integrates numerous guest lectures from industry experts, policymakers, and economists. These talks provide students with insights into current industry trends, challenges in economic policymaking, and opportunities for innovation in economics. Guest lectures serve as a bridge between academic knowledge and real-world application.

Mini Projects

  • Each semester includes a mini project where students are expected to independently research and analyse economic issues. These projects allow students to focus on specific topics such as business analysis, econometrics, government policy analysis, or market trends. Mini projects enhance their ability to conduct independent research, collect data, and present findings.

Hands-On Software Training

  • The program includes rigorous hands-on training in statistical and econometric software such as STATA, Python, R, SPSS, JMP, and Tableau. This practical approach equips students with the technical skills needed to conduct data analysis, economic forecasting, and policy evaluations. Familiarity with such tools also enhances employability in data-driven roles.
03

Aptitude Tests

  • Regular aptitude tests are included in the curriculum to assess students’ analytical, logical reasoning, and problem-solving abilities. These tests prepare students for competitive exams and enhance their readiness for roles that require quantitative and analytical expertise.

Internships and Dissertation

  • The program culminates with internships and a dissertation. Internships are designed to give students practical exposure in fields such as economic consulting, financial analysis, policy research, or business analysis. The dissertation, on the other hand, allows students to conduct in-depth research on a topic of their choice, demonstrating their ability to apply theoretical concepts to real-world challenges.

Career Prospects

Corporate Sector Opportunities

Economic Advisor

Provide expert economic insights and analysis for businesses or governmental bodies to guide decision-making.

Business Consultant

Advise companies on improving their business strategies by analysing market trends and economic conditions.

Business Associate

Support business operations by coordinating between different teams and analysing business performance.

Financial Advisor

Offer financial planning, investment management, and economic advice to individuals or corporations.

Risk Analyst

Analyse potential risks to businesses, including market, credit, or operational risks, and help mitigate them.

Research Scholar

Conduct in-depth research on economic topics in academic or research institutions, contributing to scholarly work.

Business Analyst

Analyse business processes, market trends, and financial data to provide actionable insights for companies.

Forex Dealer

Specialize in foreign exchange markets, managing currency trades and investments for firms or clients.

Economic Journalist

Report on economic trends, policies, and financial news for media outlets, helping to inform the public.

Public Sector Opportunities

  • Indian Economic Services (IES): Serve as a government economist, formulating and analysing national economic policies.
  • SEBI Grade B Officers: Work with the Securities and Exchange Board of India, regulating the securities market and protecting investors.
  • Specialist Officer in NABARD and SIDBI: Focus on agricultural development and small industries financing through policy and financial management.
  • RBI Grade B Services: Play a key role in India’s central banking system, managing monetary policy and financial regulations.
  • Indian Administrative Services (IAS): Join the civil services, handling public administration and economic policymaking at various governmental levels.
