B.Com (Hons) in International Finance and Accounting from PES University is a four-year Bachelor’s degree integrated with the curriculum for the ACCA (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants) Professional Qualification. PES university is the only university in India to secure 9 subject exemption in the launch year, from Academic year 2021.

This comprehensive global bachelor’s programme is exquisitely crafted to strengthen global careers, establish exceptional knowledge, and bridge the talent gap in the fields of commerce and finance. The programme allows students to pursue global careers in various technical areas, including audit and assurance, corporate reporting, financial management, tax and governance.

Exemptions: 9 subject exemptions along with B Com (H), Four papers to be attempted on ACCA portal during the graduation/within three years of graduation.

Why ACCA?

The growth of employment opportunities is set to surge across sectors like finance, economics, and commerce, driven by AI integration as highlighted by McKinsey and PWC. With India's rapid economic expansion, professionals equipped with techno-commerce skills and globally recognized qualifications like ACCA are well-positioned to thrive in the evolving job market.

ACCA qualification will help a student to seek a business-relevant, first-choice qualification with the aptitude, talent, and ambition to pursue a fulfilling career in accountancy, finance, or management anywhere in the world.

As a chartered accountants' community, ACCA has over 2,33,000 members and 5,36,000 students at various stages of their careers, served by a network of 110 offices and active centres. The ACCA qualification is recognised and highly respected, especially in the fields of investment banking, management, and consulting.

Programme Differentiators

Our B.Com (Hons) with ACCA program has a Curriculum Structure which is uniquely designed to integrate the academic rigors of a traditional commerce degree with the professional qualifications offered by ACCA. Core subjects in accounting, finance, economics, and business management are augmented with specialized ACCA modules covering topics such as financial reporting, taxation, audit and assurance, and strategic business leadership. Program benefits include 9 paper exemptions in knowledge and skill level from ACCA.

The Teaching-Learning process in these programs combines theoretical instruction with practical training aimed at preparing students for the rigorous ACCA examinations. Lectures, seminars, workshops, and case studies are commonly utilized to enhance students' understanding of complex accounting and business concepts. Additionally, mock exams and practice questions help students familiarize themselves with the format and requirements of the ACCA assessments.

Assessments within the program typically include a mix of traditional exams, coursework assignments, practical exercises, and mock ACCA exams. These assessments aim to evaluate students' comprehension of accounting principles, their ability to apply these principles in real-world scenarios, and their readiness to undertake the ACCA professional exams.

Students enrolled in our B.Com ACCA program benefit from the Exposure to industry-standard accounting practices and international accounting standards. Internship opportunities, guest lectures by industry professionals, and networking events provide valuable insights into the profession and help students build professional connections.

  • Invite industry experts and professionals to deliver guest lectures, providing students with valuable insights into current industry practices, trends, and challenges.

Workshops and Seminars

  • Conduct workshops and seminars focused on ACCA exam preparation, practical accounting software usage, and soft skills development. These sessions bridge the gap between academia and industry expectations.

Professional Certification Support

  • Offer dedicated support for ACCA exam preparation, including study groups, practice exams, and mentoring. This ensures that students are well-prepared for certification, enhancing their employability.

Internships and Practical Experience

  • Facilitate internships and practical experiences with industry partners, allowing students to apply classroom learning to real-world situations, thus enhancing their practical skills and professional acumen.

Technology Integration

  • Leverage modern teaching technologies, simulations, and accounting software to familiarize students with the tools and technologies used in the industry, preparing them for a tech-savvy work environment.

Research Projects

  • Encourage students to undertake research projects related to emerging trends in commerce and management accounting. This promotes independent thinking and a deeper understanding of evolving industry dynamics.

Continuous Assessment

  • Implement a system of continuous assessment through quizzes, assignments, and presentations, providing regular feedback to students and promoting a consistent and thorough understanding of the subjects.

Placement Opportunities & Career Assistance

Placements have always been the USP of PES University. Professional placement assistance will be provided through the centralised placement cell of the university, along with organised training in soft skills, aptitude, etiquette, resume building, etc., from the first semester itself to instill the necessary qualities for students to succeed in campus placements.

IN ADDITION, THE FOLLOWING WILL BE EXTENDED TO ALL OUR B.COM (HONS) WITH ACCA STUDENTS:

RESUME BUILDING AND INTERVIEW WORKSHOPS: Career assistance includes workshops on resume building, interview skills, and effective communication, preparing students for the competitive job market.

MOCK INTERVIEWS: The university conducts mock interview sessions to help students gain confidence and polish their interview techniques. Feedback from industry experts is provided to help students refine their skills.

LEVERAGE OF OUR ALUMNI NETWORK: Graduates benefit from a strong and active alumni network that provides mentorship, networking opportunities, and insights into various career paths. Alumni often play a pivotal role in connecting current students with employment opportunities.

ACCA EXAM SUPPORT: Recognising the significance of the ACCA qualification, the university offers dedicated support for ACCA exam preparation, including study materials, practice exams, and guidance from experienced faculty.

INTERNSHIP OPPORTUNITIES: The university facilitates internships with leading organisations, allowing students to gain practical experience, apply classroom knowledge, and establish connections within the industry.

CAREER COUNSELING: The university offers personalised career counselling services to help students identify their strengths, interests, and career goals. This guidance assists students in making informed decisions about their professional journeys
