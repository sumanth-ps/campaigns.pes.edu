        Eligibility & admission

        How to get in.

        The same entry route applies to all three programs, so you can decide between them after you register.

        Who can apply

        50% aggregate in 10+2 or equivalent, from any stream and any recognised board.

        Commerce is not a prerequisite — students from science, arts and vocational streams are all eligible on the same criterion.

        Documents usually required

        • Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets
        • Transfer and migration certificates
        • Government-issued photo ID
        • Passport-size photographs
        • Caste or category certificate, if applicable
        • Confirm the exact list with admissions for your intake

        01

        Register online

        Create your application at admissions.pes.edu and choose BBA as your program of interest.

        02

        Admission process

        Admissions will confirm the selection format and dates for your intake.

        03

        Submit documents

        Upload marksheets and identity documents for verification against the eligibility criteria.

        04

        Receive your offer

        Successful candidates receive an offer naming the program, with a window to confirm.

        05

        Accept and enrol

        Confirm your seat, complete the fee formalities and join orientation ahead of classes.

        Dates, intake and fee details change each cycle. Call 080-10-297297 or email admissions@pes.edu for the current year's schedule.

        Applications open

        It takes about ten minutes to start your application. Start now.
