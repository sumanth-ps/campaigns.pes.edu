Curriculum Overview

The B.Com (Hons) program at PES University is designed to provide students with a comprehensive education in commerce, integrating theoretical knowledge with practical exposure and industry engagement. The curriculum spans across eight semesters over four years, offering a balanced mix of academic rigor and hands-on experience to prepare students for a successful career in commerce and finance. The B.Com (Hons) program is a four-year course. Students who exit after three years will receive a B.Com degree, while only those completing all four years will be awarded the B.Com (Hons) degree.

  • Curriculum Design: The B.Com (Hons) program at PES University is carefully structured to provide a balance between academic learning and practical experience. In the first six semesters, students work through case studies, worksheets, and research papers to develop critical thinking and analytical skills. Hands-on learning is reinforced through bootstrap projects, hackathons, and guest talks led by industry experts. Additionally, club activities, industrial visits, and summits offer exposure to real-world business environments. By the end of the sixth semester, students have completed internships, providing them with direct industry experience. In the final two semesters, students participate in live projects and complete major academic projects, applying their skills in real-time business contexts. Specialized papers and aptitude tests help solidify their understanding of commerce and finance.
  • Special Topics: PES University offers unique special topics that allow students to gain expertise in critical and emerging areas. Topics such as strength and conditioning/yoga promote holistic well-being, while commodity market trading and financial modelling build practical business skills. Students also gain knowledge in EXIM management, Six Sigma quality control, and supply chain analytics, as well as emerging technologies like blockchain and cryptocurrency. These special topics ensure that students are well-prepared for diverse career paths and can adapt to new industry trends.
  • Industry-Standard Tools: Students are trained in essential tools used in the industry, including MS Excel, Tableau, JMP, SPSS, Tally, R, SQL, and Python. These tools are integrated into the curriculum to ensure that students are proficient in data analysis, financial modeling, and decision-making. This hands-on training ensures that graduates are ready to meet the demands of the business world.
  • Professional Exposure: The B.Com (Hons) program at PES University offers extensive opportunities for professional exposure. Certificate programs and hands-on experience with data tools like Python, Tableau, and Excel help students build specialized skills. Hackathons, studyathons, workshops, and summits provide collaborative learning environments where students can innovate and solve real-world challenges. Industrial visits and guest talks with industry professionals ensure students gain insights into current business practices, while research orientation encourages them to publish academic work. This comprehensive approach ensures that students graduate with a well-rounded education and are prepared to thrive in the professional world.
