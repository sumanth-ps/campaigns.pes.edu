PES University Loyalty Program

1. Loyalty Program for Current PES Employees

Benefit: 30% fee concession annually for all years of study.

Eligibility:

  • Children of full-time current employees on the payroll of PES University.
  • The parent must have been employed at PES University continuously for at least 5 years at the time of the candidate’s admission.
  • Applicable Programs: All programs of study at PES University.

2. Loyalty Program for Students from PES Institutions

Benefit: 20% fee concession annually for all years of study.

Eligibility:

  • Students who have graduated from any PES Institution or sister concern, including:
    • Amaatra Academy
    • SSM Public School
    • PES Public School, Chittoor
    • PESIMSR, Kuppam
    • PES University (Hanumanth Nagar Campus,
    • Ring Road Campus, Electronic City Campus)
    • Applicable Programs: All programs of study at PES University.
  • Students who are currently studying at PES and willing to secure admissions in future for a UG / PG program at PES will also be eligible for this benefit.

3. Loyalty Program for Siblings of Current Students & PES Alumni

Benefit: 10% fee concession annually for all years of study.

Eligibility:

  • Siblings of Current Students & PES Alumni.
  • Applicable Programs: All programs of study at PES University.

4. Loyalty Program for Children of PES Alumni

Benefit: 10% fee concession annually for all years of study.

Eligibility:

  • Children of PES Alumni.
  • Applicable Programs: All programs of study at PES University.
