B.Com (Hons) with CMA program at PES University, Bangalore – a dynamic blend of academic excellence and professional expertise. This innovative undergraduate course is designed to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of commerce while offering a specialized focus on Certified Management Accountancy (CMA). Students will get the B.Com (strategic finance) after the success full completing of the four years program.

B.Com integrated with CMA program seamlessly merges the foundational aspects of a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) with the strategic insights of Certified Management Accountant (CMA) certification, provided by the Institute of Management Accountants (IMA), USA. The course is globally recognized in 150 countries, with over 100,000 certifications earned worldwide. It’s an advanced- level credential appropriate for accountants and financial professionals in business. The B.Com (Hons)(Strategic Finance) program will consist of 160 credits spread over four years and eight semesters. Students will be interning in the corporate sector for two semesters, in the third and fourth years, respectively.

Our dedicated faculty, industry-aligned curriculum, and state-of-the-art facilities create an enriching learning environment for aspiring professionals. Graduates of this program will emerge with a strong foundation in commerce, coupled with the prestigious CMA qualification, positioning them as sought-after professionals in the world of finance and accounting. Prepare for a successful career in commerce with the B.Com (Hons) with CMA program at PES University – where academic excellence meets professional acumen

Curriculum Structure

CMA Part 1: Financial Planning, Performance, and Analytics

This part focuses on foundational elements of financial management, providing students with essential skills in the following areas:

External Financial Reporting Decisions: Emphasizes financial reporting standards, preparing financial statements, and analysing external reports.

Cost Management: Focuses on budgeting, cost control, and effective management of operational costs.

Internal Controls: Covers risk management, internal auditing practices, and ensuring regulatory compliance.

Planning, Budgeting, and Forecasting: Teaches strategic financial planning, revenue forecasting, and cash flow management.

Technology and Analytics: Involves the use of financial data analytics and modern technological tools for decision-making.

Performance Management: Helps in evaluating organizational performance through KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) and strategic tools.

CMA Part 2: Strategic Financial Management

This part advances the financial expertise of students, focusing on strategic decision-making and financial management.

Financial Statement Analysis: Develops skills to analyze and interpret financial statements for decision-making.

Corporate Finance: Focuses on managing a company’s financial resources, including capital structuring and financing decisions.

Risk Management: Teaches techniques to identify, assess, and mitigate financial risks in business operations.

Investment Decisions: Guides in making informed investment choices and managing portfolios.

Decision Analysis: Involves strategic decision-making based on financial analysis and forecasting models.

Professional Ethics: Emphasizes ethical practices and standards in financial management and decision-making.

Placement Opportunities & Career Assistance

Placements have always been the USP of PES University. Professional placement assistance will be provided through the centralised placement cell of the university, along with organised training in soft skills, aptitude, etiquette, resume building, etc., from the first semester itself to instill the necessary qualities for students to succeed in campus placements.

In addition, the following will be extended to all our B.Com (Hons) with CMA students:

  • Resume building and Interview workshops: Career assistance includes workshops on resume building, interview skills, and effective communication, preparing students for the competitive job market.
  • Mock Interviews: The university conducts mock interview sessions to help students gain confidence and polish their interview techniques. Feedback from industry experts is provided to help students refine their skills.
  • Leverage of our Alumni Network: Graduates benefit from a strong and active alumni network that provides mentorship, networking opportunities, and insights into various career paths. Alumni often play a pivotal role in connecting current students with employment opportunities.
  • CMA Exam Support: Recognising the significance of the CMA qualification, the university offers dedicated support for CMA exam preparation, including study materials, practice exams, and guidance from experienced faculty.
  • Internship Opportunities: The university facilitates internships with leading organisations, allowing students to gain practical experience, apply classroom knowledge, and establish connections within the industry.
  • Career Counselling: The university offers personalised career counselling services to help students identify their strengths, interests, and career goals. This guidance assists students in making informed decisions about their professional journeys.
