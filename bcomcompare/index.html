Overview

What this program actually is.

B.Com / B.Com (Hons)

The commerce degree that opens every door.

The Bachelor of Commerce programme is designed to equip you with accounting and financial skills and build competencies required to succeed in the field of commerce. The three/four year programme will equip you with knowledge of accounting, principles, export and import laws, economic policies and other aspects that influence trade and business.

More about the program

B.Com is a renowned undergraduate course with many job opportunities. It lays a strong foundation for prospective learners. The B.Com degree course aims to teach students analytical abilities, financial management, and business skills by emphasising conceptual topics. After 3 years students who exit the program will get a BCom degree and those who continue and finish in 4 years will be awarded a BCom (Hons) degree.

            Eligibility & admission

            How to get in.

            The same entry route applies to all five programs, so you can decide between them after you register.

            Who can apply

            50% aggregate in 10+2 or equivalent, from any stream and any recognised board.

            Commerce is not a prerequisite — students from science, arts and vocational streams are all eligible on the same criterion. The same requirement applies to all five programs.

            Documents usually required

            • Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets
            • Transfer and migration certificates
            • Government-issued photo ID
            • Passport-size photographs
            • Caste or category certificate, if applicable
            • Confirm the exact list with admissions for your intake

            01

            Register online

            Create your application at admissions.pes.edu and choose B.Com as your program of interest.

            02

            PES Entrance Process

            PES lists the PES Entrance Process as the admissions route for these commerce degrees. Admissions will confirm the format and dates for your intake.

            03

            Submit documents

            Upload marksheets and identity documents for verification against the eligibility criteria.

            04

            Receive your offer

            Successful candidates receive an offer naming the program and campus, with a window to confirm.

            05

            Accept and enrol

            Confirm your seat, complete the fee formalities and join orientation ahead of classes.

            Dates, intake and fee details change each cycle. Call 080-10-297297 or email admissions@pes.edu for the current year's schedule.

            Before you apply.

            The things applicants and parents ask us most often. Anything else, the admissions office picks up on 080-10-297297.

            No. All five programs accept 50% aggregate in 10+2 or equivalent from any stream and any recognised board. The first-year syllabus is built to bring students from science, arts and vocational backgrounds to the same footing.
            B.Com is the three-year exit. B.Com (Hons) is the four-year degree — you leave with a B.Com if you exit after three years, and with Honours only if you complete all four. The ACCA, CMA and CFA programs are four-year Honours degrees from the start.
            Each embeds a global professional qualification inside the degree. ACCA exempts nine papers and is delivered with ACCA, UK. CMA integrates both parts of the IMA (USA) qualification and awards a B.Com (Hons) in Strategic Finance. CFA covers all three levels of the CFA curriculum within the four years. The B.Com (General/Hons) stays general, with subjects aligned to CA and optional afternoon CA training, leaving every professional route open.
            B.Com (Hons) in Finance and Accounting (CA Embedded) is a four-year Honours degree at the Ring Road campus with CA subjects incorporated into the syllabus. Degree classes run 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM, followed by exclusive CA coaching from 1:45 PM to 5:30 PM. The CA qualification itself is awarded by ICAI, whose examinations students take separately.
            The professional syllabus is integrated into the degree rather than taught as separate coaching. For the CFA program, PES states that no additional tutoring charges are levied. Confirm the current fee position for each program with the admissions office.
            Nine — all three papers at the Knowledge level and all six at the Skills level. Students need to clear the corresponding subjects while pursuing the B.Com (Hons) with ACCA in order to claim the exemption.
            Registration for all five programs runs through admissions.pes.edu. PES lists the PES Entrance Process as the admissions route for these commerce degrees; the admissions office will confirm the current format, dates and any interview stage for the intake you are applying to.
            B.Com and B.Com (Hons) run at the Ring Road campus, and B.Com also runs at Electronic City. The ACCA, CMA and CFA programs run at the Ring Road campus only.
            It depends on the program. The CMA program places students in the corporate sector for two internship semesters, in the third and fourth years. The CFA program runs three: a social internship with an NGO in semester three, an audit internship in semester six and a final internship in semester eight. The ACCA program carries an internship, placement or audit-firm term in semester eight.
