Overview

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

The Master of Business Administration (MBA) program at PES University is a two-year, globally recognised course designed to cultivate leadership, analytical, and managerial skills. With its rigorous curriculum, the MBA program integrates academic learning and real-world exposure, empowering graduates to excel in diverse industries.

Ranked as the #1 University in Karnataka and 24th among India’s top management institutes (Times of India B-School Rankings), PES University offers unparalleled academic and professional development opportunities.

Atleast 50% in Bachelor’s degree from any recognized University. Test scores in PES Entrance Test/ CAT/ MAT/ CMAT/ ATMA accepted.

How We Teach

Pedagogy

01

Case-Based Learning

Integrate case studies drawn from real business scenarios to connect theoretical concepts with practical application. This method develops analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and decision-making under uncertainty.

Research and Presentations

Encourage students to write research papers and deliver presentations on contemporary issues, enhancing research aptitude, critical thinking, public speaking, and emotional resilience.

02

Hackathons, Studyathons, and Readathons

Promote collaborative and competitive learning environments through intensive events that nurture innovation, deep analysis, and reading beyond the curriculum.

Workshops and Certificate Programs

Conduct workshops and specialised certificate courses on leadership, strategy, and emerging technologies to build industry-relevant skills and keep students aligned with current trends.

03

Interactive Class Participation

Foster meaningful classroom engagement by encouraging respectful expression of ideas, critical dialogue, and peer learning, thereby enriching the academic experience.

Networking with CXOs

Facilitate student interaction with over 100+ CXOs through talks and networking events, offering real-world insights, leadership exposure, and valuable professional connections. From the Dean

Message from the Dean

Welcome to the MBA Programme at PES University!

At PES University, we combine experiential learning with innovative pedagogy to develop future-ready leaders. Our MBA curriculum is built around case studies, research projects, presentations, and meaningful class participation - each designed to sharpen decision-making, critical thinking, and leadership skills.

Located in Bengaluru - India’s Silicon Valley - our campus is at the heart of a thriving ecosystem of IT, retail, hospitality, manufacturing, and start-ups. Students benefit from deep industry engagement through CXO Talks, hackathons, internships, bootstrap events, and corporate visits.

Rigorous academic audits and our SOP-driven curriculum design ensure consistency, quality, and relevance. Flagship initiatives like the Capstone Project empower students to solve real-world business challenges.

Our faculty are domain experts and passionate mentors who champion research and academic excellence. With access to Centres of Excellence and cutting-edge analytical tools, students are trained to adopt a research-based approach and emerge as insightful professionals.

I invite you to explore our campus, engage with our vibrant learning community, and join our growing network of alumni who are shaping the business world.
