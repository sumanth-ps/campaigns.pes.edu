Overview

B.Sc. (Hons) Dialysis Therapy Technology

Embark on a rewarding journey with the B.Sc. (Hons) Dialysis Therapy Technology program at PES University, a comprehensive course designed to equip students with the essential skills and knowledge needed to excel in the field of dialysis therapy.

As the world grapples with increasing cases of renal ailments, the demand for qualified dialysis professionals continues to surge. This program stands as a beacon of excellence, offering a robust curriculum that blends theoretical knowledge with practical experience, ensuring students are well-prepared to navigate the real-world challenges of dialysis care.

Students will delve into a range of subjects including renal anatomy and physiology, haemodialysis principles and procedures, and patient care, among others. The program emphasizes hands-on training, allowing students to gain invaluable experience in state-of-the-art dialysis facilities under the guidance of seasoned professionals. Graduates will emerge as competent dialysis technologists, ready to make a meaningful impact on patients’ lives by providing exceptional dialysis care and contributing to the advancement of renal healthcare.

Join the B.Sc. (Hons) Dialysis Therapy Technology program and navigate the world of dialysis care, steering progress and innovation in this vital healthcare sector. Your path to a fulfilling and impactful career in dialysis therapy begins here.

Is It For You?

Who should pursue this Program?

Our B.Sc. (Hons) Dialysis Therapy Technology program is tailored for individuals who are passionate about making a significant impact in healthcare, specifically in the field of renal care. It is an ideal choice for those who have a strong foundation in the sciences and exhibit a keen interest in medical technology and patient care. The program is also suited for individuals seeking a hands-on, practical learning experience, as it offers ample opportunities for real-world training in state-of-the-art dialysis facilities.

Aspiring healthcare professionals who are committed to lifelong learning, and who wish to stay abreast with the latest advancements in dialysis technology, will find this program to be a valuable stepping stone. It is designed to equip students with the comprehensive knowledge and technical skills necessary to excel as dialysis technologists, providing exceptional care to patients undergoing dialysis and contributing to the betterment of renal healthcare. If you are driven by a desire to improve the lives of individuals suffering from renal ailments and are eager to embark on a rewarding and fulfilling career, the B.Sc. (Hons) Dialysis Therapy Technology program is the perfect pathway for you.

Curriculum

Year-wise Curriculum Structure

  • Basic Anatomy (Including Histology)
  • Physiology
  • Biochemistry
  • Pathology I (Clinical Pathology, Haematology & Blood Banking)
  • Microbiology

  • Applied anatomy and physiology related to dialysis technology
  • Pharmacology related to Haemodialysis and Peritoneal Dialysis
  • Concepts of renal disease: Clinical manifestation and its management
  • Applied aspects of pathology & microbiology
  • Basics of Renal Dialysis Technology

  • Applied Dialysis Technology Paper 1
  • Applied Dialysis Technology Paper 2

*In addition to the main subjects mentioned above, there will be other additional subjects/ electives as a part of the curriculum as well as practical tests and lab components every year.

**Curriculum structure is subject to change without prior notice based on the recurring norms of our university.

Careers

Future Career Prospects after completing the Program

Some of the future career prospects for the graduates of B.Sc. (Hons) Dialysis Therapy Technology students are as following

Dialysis Technologist/Technician

Graduates can work in hospitals, clinics, and dialysis centres, operating dialysis machines, monitoring patients during dialysis procedures, and ensuring adherence to safety standards and protocols.

Renal Care Specialist

Graduates can specialize in renal care, providing dedicated support and care to patients with kidney disorders, and assisting in their treatment and recovery process.

Clinical Coordinator

With experience, graduates can assume roles as clinical coordinators, overseeing the operations of dialysis units, ensuring compliance with healthcare regulations, and managing staff and patient care.

Healthcare Consultant

Graduates can work as consultants, advising dialysis centres and healthcare facilities on best practices, technology implementation, and patient care improvement.

Medical Equipment Marketing and Sales Specialist

Leveraging their technical knowledge, graduates can work with medical equipment companies, aiding in the sales, marketing, and support of dialysis-related products and technologies.

Research and Development

Graduates can engage in research, contributing to advancements in dialysis technology, renal care treatments, and healthcare innovations.

Educators/Trainers

With expertise in dialysis therapy technology, graduates can take up roles as educators or trainers, imparting knowledge and skills to aspiring dialysis technicians and healthcare professionals at higher education institutions or universities.
