Bachelor of Business Administration: BBA 3 year and BBA (Hons) 4-year Program is one of the best undergraduate programs available nationally. Our Story

Our Legacy | PES @ 50

For over 50 years, PES University has been a pioneer in shaping future-ready professionals and leaders. From a humble beginning with just 40 students to a thriving community of 25,000+ students, our journey reflects an unwavering commitment to quality education, innovation and excellence.

At the heart of this remarkable journey are the core values of PES — Perseverance, Excellence and Service. These values continue to guide everything we do, inspiring our students and faculty to learn continuously, challenge conventions and strive for meaningful impact.

PES University has consistently embraced emerging technologies, innovative academic programmes and evolving industry trends, ensuring that our students are prepared not merely for the opportunities of today, but for the possibilities of tomorrow.

How It Is Built

Curriculum Design

  1. 01

    Our students graduate with the ability to adapt to an intellectual and technologically changing environment. The BBA program at PES University provides students with a comprehensive education aimed at preparing them for the challenges of the business world. BBA Program Curriculum is well mapped with National & International colleges and universities. The curriculum is regularly updated every year to meet industry demands, ensuring students possess current and relevant knowledge.

  2. 02

    Throughout the program, students delve into core subjects such as Accounting, Economics, Finance, Marketing, Organizational Behaviour, Project Management, Supply Chain Management, Productions and Operations Management,Design Thinking and Innovation Management , establishing a strong foundational knowledge base. From the 3rd semester onwards, elective courses like Marketing, Finance, International Business, Family Business and Entrepreneurship, and Human Resource allow students to tailor their studies to their career interests. The curriculum is well planned in a sequence of instructions consisting of Units meant for developing employability and Skills competencies of students opting for Skills essential for overall development.

  3. 03

    Practical learning is emphasized, with students gaining hands-on experience using software tools like Python, Tableau, Excel and PowerBI for data analysis. Innovative teaching methods, including worksheet based hands on assignements and real-life case studies, enhance problem-solving and critical thinking skills. Additionally, an Honors track (4 Years Program) with 160+ credits is available for students seeking specialized study of business concepts embedded with Analytical and Technical skills and practices that will help seek admissions to top foreign universities or ivy league colleges.
